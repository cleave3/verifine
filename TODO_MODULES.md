# Verifine: Complete Accounting Suite Roadmap

This document outlines the missing modules and sub-systems required to transform Verifine from a core ledger into a comprehensive, professional-grade accounting suite.

## 1. Bank Reconciliation (High Priority)
- [ ] Upload bank statements (CSV/OFX/QIF).
- [ ] Direct Bank Feed integration [via Plaid (international) and mono (local, Nigeria and Africa) ].
- [ ] Matching engine for statement lines vs. ledger entries.
- [ ] Discrepancy reporting and adjustment workflows.

## 2. Fixed Assets & Depreciation
- [ ] Asset Registry (cost, date of purchase, category).
- [ ] Automatic Depreciation calculation (Straight Line, Reducing Balance).
- [ ] Automated Journal Entry posting for monthly depreciation.
- [ ] Asset disposal and gain/loss on sale tracking.

## 3. Inventory Management
- [ ] SKU/Product catalog.
- [ ] Stock level tracking (In/Out/On Hand).
- [ ] Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) automation linked to Invoices.
- [ ] Multi-warehouse support.

## 4. Tax Engine & Compliance
- [ ] VAT/GST/Sales Tax automated calculation.
- [ ] Tax Liability reporting (Inputs vs. Outputs).
- [ ] Tax adjustments and manual overrides.
- [ ] Localized tax filing exports.

## 5. Budgeting & Forecasting
- [ ] Annual/Monthly budget setting per account.
- [ ] Actual vs. Budget performance reporting.
- [ ] Controller approval workflows for budget changes.

## 6. Purchase & Sales Operations
- [ ] Quotes and Estimates (Convert to Invoice).
- [ ] Sales Orders tracking.
- [ ] Purchase Orders and Requisitions (Convert to Bill).

## 7. Dimensional Accounting (Project/Dept)
- [ ] Transaction tagging (Dimensions/Classes).
- [ ] P&L and Balance Sheet reporting filtered by Project or Department.

## 8. Expense Reimbursements
- [ ] Employee out-of-pocket expense submission.
- [ ] Receipt/Image attachment support.
- [ ] Multi-stage approval for employee claims.

## 9. Year-End Closing Workflow
- [ ] Automatic zero-out of Income/Expense accounts.
- [ ] Transfer of Net Income to Retained Earnings.
- [ ] Prior-period lock enforcement across all entities.
